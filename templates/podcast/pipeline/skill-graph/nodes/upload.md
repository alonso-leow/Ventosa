# Node skill: upload [human, upload list]
**Where:** labelling app ▸ Queues & uploads.
**Human does:** uploads the episode file, transcript and chapters to the podcast host (copy buttons for title, notes, tags), pastes the episode URL, presses **mark uploaded**.
**Events:** `publish.uploaded` (url), `publish.unuploaded`.
**AI may:** check that the chapters in the notes match the uploaded file.
