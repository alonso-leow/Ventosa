# Tone

The law for every AI step. Replace this file with your own voice. Every node skill says "follow the content rules in tone.md".

## Writing policy
1. `meta/meta-script.md` is the true source for the videos' voice.
2. Allowed: rephrase, or add paragraphs in the same style, tone and pace.

## Structure (every video)
- Hook first (≤40 s), then the sponsor line from `workspace.json` if it has one, then three acts, then a call to action.

## Content rules
- Facts only from `research/`. Nothing unbuilt presented as built.
- Add your own rules here (sources you trust or refuse, topics to avoid, music policy).

## Rendering
Where inserts and renders come from, and which tools make them (e.g. HyperFrames, a game engine, a DAW). The `edit` node reads this.
