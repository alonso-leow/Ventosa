# 3D scenes (K-3d)

A 3D step produces models, stills or short clips for motion graphics: a logo turning, a product on a pedestal, a diagram in depth, a camera move through a set.

## Tool options
| Tool | Use when | Output | Wiring |
|---|---|---|---|
| three.js scene inside a HyperFrames or Remotion scene | simple objects, extruded type, particles, camera moves, everything live in the HTML | frames from the render engine | no extra tool; in `motion_source` |
| Blender (headless script, or Blender MCP) | modelled scenes, materials, lighting, physics, high-quality stills or clips | PNG sequence or MP4 in `assets/renders/` | workspace tool `blender -b scene.blend -P render.py -- ...` or the Blender MCP connection |
| Generated mesh (image-to-3D services, including those reachable through a media-generation MCP) | a prop from a reference image, no modelling skill | GLB/glTF into `assets/models/` | `gen_media` node, then this node cleans and stages it |
| Existing assets (own models, licensed libraries) | the human has the model | glTF/GLB/FBX | copy to `assets/models/` with licence recorded |

## Contract
Reads: the shot plan lines marked 3D, `K-animations`, `K-motion`, `K-render`, style sheet in `assets/refs/`.
Writes: `assets/models/<ID>-<name>.glb`, scene sources in `assets/scenes3d/<ID>/`, renders in `assets/renders/<ID>-3d-<beat>.mp4` or `.png`, and `assets/scenes3d/<ID>/notes.md` (tool, version, seed, camera, units, licence of every asset, render time).

## Method
Block out first (primitives, camera, lights), then materials, then motion. Plan in seconds and fps from the shot plan; loop-friendly where it will sit under speech. Keep the camera path simple and motivated. Render a low-sample preview (cheap) before the final. Use a transparent background (PNG sequence, WebM alpha or ProRes 4444) when the clip will be composited.

## Gates
- Licences: every model, texture, HDRI and font has a recorded licence; no scraped assets.
- Installs (Blender, Python packages, add-ons, MCP servers) go through the install gate (`decision_ask`); never run an add-on's or downloaded scene's script blindly. A `.blend` file can run Python: open untrusted files with auto-run disabled.
- Paid or cloud generation, hosted rendering and image-to-3D services: estimate first, ask with provider, cost and what is sent.
- Never send private images, faces of real people (other than the host) or unpublished material to a generator.
- Heavy renders: ask when the estimate exceeds about 10 minutes or needs the GPU for long; do not block the queue silently.
Done: `step-done <ID> scene_3d "n models, m renders"`.
