# Structure and pacing (K-explainer)

Timing aids for scripts and shot plans. They never override `tone.md` or an approved script.

## Structures
- **Problem, agitate, solve (about 60 s):** problem 10 s, agitate 10 s, solution 15 s, how it works 20 s (three steps), call to action 5 s. Word budget roughly 25 / 25 / 35 / 50 / 12.
- **Before, after, bridge (about 90 s):** current pain 15 s, ideal result 15 s, bridge 40 s, proof 10 s, call to action 10 s.
- **One-feature short (about 30 s):** hook 3 s, the feature solving one problem 15 s, result 7 s, call to action 5 s.

## Speed
Spoken words per minute: 150 normal, 120 technical, 170 energetic short-form, 100 for young children. One scene per message. Establishing shot 3-5 s, demonstration 5-8 s, on-screen stat or text 3-4 s, transition under 1 s, closing card 3-5 s.

## Writing for the ear
Sentences under 15 words, active voice, one idea per sentence and per visual beat. Read it aloud; rewrite what sounds stiff. For synthetic speech: a period is a normal pause, a comma a short one, an ellipsis a long one, an exclamation mark adds energy, a question mark lifts the end.

## Music under speech
Bed 20-30% under narration, duck 6-12 dB while the voice plays, swell in the first 3 s and at the call to action, no vocals.

## Target length by format
Teaser 15-30 s · site demo 60-90 s · feature explainer 90-120 s · tutorial 2-5 min · investor video 2-3 min.

## Mistakes and fixes
Too wordy (cap at 150 wpm) · no hook in 3 s (open on the problem or a surprising number) · picture trails the words (show it as, or just before, it is said) · music too loud (duck it) · no captions (most social video plays muted) · too many ideas (one message per video).

## Talking-head recipe
Analyse takes, transcribe, find silences, cut, add captions, title, lower third, music at low level, call to action. For a clip from a long take, pick start and end on sentence boundaries and shift caption times by the start.
